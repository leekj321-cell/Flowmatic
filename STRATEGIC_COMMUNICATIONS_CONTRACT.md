# Flowmatic Strategic Communications Contract

**Status:** MANDATORY / FAIL-CLOSED  
**Scope:** investor pitch, IR deck, business plan, grant/application narrative, proposal, company profile, market/competition analysis, executive report, public website strategy copy, and any other external Flowmatic strategic communication.

This contract is the writing authority for external strategic communication. It is intentionally broader than the website-specific `INVESTOR_COPY_CONTRACT.md`.

## 1. Operating principle

Flowmatic external materials use **analytical report language**, not conversational presentation language.

The objective is to present:

- what the structure is,
- what evidence supports it,
- what residual problem remains,
- what Flowmatic's position is,
- what is current vs. pilot vs. target,
- and what decision follows.

The material must not narrate the act of presenting itself.

### Prohibited presentation narration

Do not use titles or body copy such as:

- 현재 작동하는 것
- 현재 작동하는 제품부터 보여드립니다
- 우리가 만들고 있는 것
- 경쟁사는 잘하고 있습니다
- 어디서 시작합니다
- 무엇을 바꿀지 보여줍니다
- 반복 검토로 시간을 잃는 품목이나 라인이 있습니까?
- See what works today
- What we are building
- Where is your team losing time?
- Start with what you already use
- Prove improvement before...

Replace them with analytical labels such as:

- 현재 구현 범위
- 현재 구현 범위 및 검증 자산
- 목표 제품 범위
- 경쟁구조 및 시장 공백
- 초기 시장 및 우선 타깃
- 제조 판단 Hand-off 구조
- Pilot 적용 범위
- Current Implementation Scope
- Target Product Scope
- Competitive Structure & Market Gap
- Market Entry Strategy
- Commercial Model
- Expansion Model

## 2. Two mandatory writing profiles

### A. Strategic Report Profile

Applies to pitch HTML, investor decks, IR, business plans, proposals, applications, executive reports, market research and competition analysis.

**Korean**
- Headings: noun phrase or analytical conclusion.
- Body: plain declarative report style: `~이다 / ~한다 / ~된다 / ~로 구성된다 / ~로 평가된다 / ~을 목표로 한다`.
- Do not use honorific presentation endings such as `~드립니다 / ~보여드립니다 / ~하겠습니다`.
- Avoid first-person and second-person framing: `우리 / 우리가 / 저희 / 여러분 / 당신`.
- Avoid rhetorical questions.
- Avoid casual or aggressive metaphors: `먹는다 / 장악한다 / 찌른다 / 갈아엎는다 / 돈이 되는`.
- Internal strategic slang may be used only in clearly marked internal notes, never in external copy.

**English**
- Use neutral analytical statements.
- Headings are category labels or analytical conclusions, not commands or questions.
- Avoid first/second person (`we / our / you / your`) in analytical copy.
- Avoid presentation narration such as `See...`, `Start...`, `What works today`, `Who uses it`.
- Use terms such as `Current Implementation Scope`, `Residual Problem`, `Flowmatic Entry Point`, `Commercial Model`.

**Arabic**
- Use formal analytical statements.
- Avoid rhetorical questions and imperative presentation language.
- Match the same information hierarchy and claim boundaries as Korean.

### B. Public Web Profile

Applies to public website headings and strategic narrative.

- Headings follow the same analytical/report-style rule as the Strategic Report Profile.
- Body copy may be customer-facing, but must remain formal, concise and non-conversational.
- Functional UI controls are exceptions: CTA buttons may use imperative labels such as `View Demo` or `파일럿 문의`.
- Rhetorical-question headings are prohibited.
- First-person promotional claims and self-congratulatory copy are prohibited.

## 3. Heading contract

Every section heading must perform one of four functions:

1. identify a strategic category,
2. state an analytical conclusion,
3. define a scope,
4. define a model or structure.

Preferred patterns:

- 현재 구현 범위 및 검증 자산
- 제조 운영 범위: 도면에서 양산까지
- 초기 시장: 기존 설비 중심의 중견 제조기업
- 경쟁구조: 기능별 솔루션 분절과 Decision-to-Action 공백
- 시장 진입 전략: 최소 단위 제조 폐루프 우선 적용
- 상업화 모델: Paid Pilot → Subscription → Expansion
- 고객 내 확장 구조: Workflow → Line → Factory
- Current Implementation Scope & Validation Assets
- Competitive Structure & Market Gap
- Decision-to-Action Positioning
- Market Entry Strategy
- Commercial Model
- Customer Expansion Model

### Heading fail conditions

A heading fails if it:
- addresses the reader,
- asks a question,
- narrates what the presentation will show,
- uses conversational endings,
- praises or attacks another company,
- uses unexplained internal jargon,
- or repeats the body instead of defining the analytical frame.

## 4. Section-role contract

Each section has exactly one primary information job.

| Section | Required question answered |
| --- | --- |
| Vision | What system is Flowmatic building? |
| Current Scope | What is implemented and demonstrable now? |
| Why Now | Why is this technically/economically feasible now? |
| Market | Which manufacturing segment and accounts fit the initial ICP? |
| Competition | How are incumbent solutions structurally organized and what hand-off remains? |
| Positioning | Which residual layer does Flowmatic occupy? |
| Entry Strategy | What is the minimum closed loop used for entry? |
| Commercial Model | How does initial validation become recurring revenue? |
| Expansion | How does one customer expand from workflow to line to factory? |

Do not repeat the same `small start → ROI → expand` message in multiple sections. State it once as strategy and once as commercial mechanics only when the information role differs.

## 5. Mandatory competition-analysis schema

Competition analysis must **not** be a list of competitors with subjective strengths/weaknesses.

Every competitor category or named competitor must be analyzed in this order:

1. **Existing Solution Structure**  
   What part of the manufacturing value chain the solution owns and how it operates.

2. **Residual Problem / Manual Handoff**  
   What decision, integration or action remains outside the solution. This must be a structural observation, not an unsupported criticism.

3. **Flowmatic Entry Point**  
   Which residual workflow Flowmatic targets and how the mechanism differs.

4. **Scale / Evidence Note**  
   Public scale indicator and source basis where relevant.

Required examples of valid competitive logic:

- Engineering / Knowledge → information retrieval remains separate from current-process action → **Knowledge → Action**
- Operations / Data → telemetry remains separate from corrective decision → **Evidence → Decision**
- Quality / Inspection → defect evidence remains separate from process correction → **Defect → Correction**
- Enterprise Stack → broad capability requires substantial integration before first value → **One Loop → ROI → Expand**

### Competition prohibited framing

Do not write:
- 경쟁사는 잘하고 있다 / 못하고 있다.
- 경쟁사의 약점은...
- 우리가 더 낫다.
- Siemens를 이긴다.
- CADDi가 못하는 것은...

Use:
- 기존 솔루션 구조
- 잔존 문제 / Manual Handoff
- Flowmatic Entry Point
- 구조적 차이
- 적용 범위 차이

### F4GE rule

F4GE is handled as a separate **partner / competitor adjacency** case because it combines Cell OS with manufacturing capacity and production assets. Do not place it mechanically in the same category as a point software solution.

## 6. Market / target-account contract

- Use `Priority Target Account`, `Illustrative Priority Account`, or equivalent.
- Never label a non-customer company as a Flowmatic customer.
- Ranking means **ICP fit + plant-level entry feasibility**, not predicted purchase probability.
- For each region, show approximately five priority accounts and a secondary target pool.
- Scale data must identify the relevant fiscal period and, when material, consolidated vs. standalone basis.
- Pitch tables use rounded values for readability; detailed source notes retain exact figures.
- Do not use target-account lists as a substitute for TAM/SAM/SOM.

## 7. Claim-state contract

Use these formal labels:

| State | Korean | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current | 현재 구현 범위 | Current Implementation Scope |
| Pilot | Pilot 검증 범위 | Pilot Validation Scope |
| Target | 목표 제품 범위 | Target Product Scope |

Bad:
- 지금 작동하는 것
- 우리가 만들고 있는 것
- What works today
- What we are building

CURRENT claims must be demonstrable.  
PILOT claims require customer-environment validation.  
TARGET claims describe long-range scope and must not be written as completed functionality.

## 8. Commercial-language contract

Preferred:
- 유료 Pilot
- Base Subscription
- Expansion Revenue
- 적용 범위 확대
- 추가 Workflow / Line / Connector
- KPI 비교
- ROI 검증

Avoid:
- 돈이 되는 연결
- 먹고 확장
- 장악
- 깔아준다
- 꽂는다
- 한번 돌려본다

External copy describes mechanisms, not internal battle slang.

## 9. Data and evidence contract

Every externally used factual number should be traceable to:
- source,
- date or fiscal period,
- population/scope,
- and measurement basis where material.

Competition sizing:
- Private software companies: valuation/funding/headcount may be the relevant scale indicator.
- Public incumbents: revenue/headcount/business-segment size may be more appropriate.
- Do not imply that heterogeneous scale indicators are directly comparable.

No unsupported performance percentage, ROI period or customer result may be invented.

## 10. Language-mixing contract

English terms may remain when they are established product/business terms:

- Factory OS
- CURRENT / PILOT / TARGET badges
- ROI
- NC / G-code / CT
- Workflow → Line → Factory
- Decision-to-Action
- Paid Pilot
- Base Subscription

Replace unnecessary hybrid slang:
- wedge → 진입 업무 / 진입 지점
- brownfield → first mention: 기존 설비 중심의 brownfield 제조환경; thereafter 기존 공장
- closed loop → 제조 폐루프 / 반복 개선 구조
- plant-level → 공장 단위

## 11. Pre-delivery quality gate

Before any external Flowmatic strategic artifact is delivered:

1. Confirm the correct profile: `strategic-report` or `public-web`.
2. Run `python tools/validate_strategic_copy.py --profile <profile> <files...>` when the artifact is text/HTML and available in the repository/runtime.
3. Review every heading for report-style compliance.
4. Remove rhetorical questions, presentation narration and first/second-person analytical framing.
5. Verify competition sections follow Structure → Residual Problem → Entry Point.
6. Verify Current / Pilot / Target boundaries.
7. Verify target-account status and financial-period notes.
8. Verify repeated messages have distinct section roles.
9. Verify no internal battle slang remains in external copy.
10. Do not deliver until hard failures are cleared.

## 12. Quality benchmark

A compliant Flowmatic strategic document should read like a combined output of:
- corporate strategy,
- manufacturing domain analysis,
- investment research,
- and product strategy.

It must not read like:
- a sales-script transcript,
- a classroom presentation,
- AI-generated explanatory prose,
- or a conversation with the reader.

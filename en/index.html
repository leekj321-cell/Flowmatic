The problem

Production preparation concentrated in a few experienced people

A new product requires coordinated decisions on processes, equipment, tools, transport and inspection. When information sits across drawings, files and individual experience, each change creates another round of checks and coordination.

Concentrating these decisions in a few specialists makes onboarding and handovers harder, while increasing the burden of managing more products and production lines.

New-product introductionJoint review of process, equipment and logistics conditions
Staff or volume changesHandover and reuse of earlier review criteria

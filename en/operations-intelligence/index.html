How Operations Intelligence works

Read, record,act, and confirm.

Record camera, NC, inspection, and operator inputs as events, then close each event with an owner, action, and confirmed completion.

  1. ObserveRead signals from real work: camera, NC code, operator input, and field data.
  2. EventizeTurn raw signals into operational events such as delay, risk, and demand.
  3. ActConnect the event to one next action: alert, review, guide, or call.
  4. ConfirmConfirm the human or equipment response and close the event.

Functional MVP

Procurement Intelligence

Extract item, specification, quantity, UOM, and context from free-text mail or chat; present candidates with confidence and reason; export confirmed items only to the existing request template.

  • No silent 1EA default for missing quantity
  • No auto-confirm on conflicting specifications
  • No arbitrary conversion of unknown pack units
  • Quantity / use-location split
  • Confirmed-only template export

Functional MVP

Resource Economics

Combine Tool, Consumable, and Labor events as Tracked Operational Cost and normalize them by production and running time.

  • Cost / Production EA
  • Cost / Good EA
  • Man-hour / 1,000 EA
  • Consumption / 1,000 EA
  • Consumption / running hour

01 · FIELD GAP

Equipment and systems exist. Judgment, handoff, and follow-up still depend on people.

Repeated entry, approval waits, and cause tracing across Excel, email, and chat delay production, quality, and logistics response.

MACHINING

Machining information does not form one process

FIELD GAP
QUALITY

Quality data is disconnected from improvement work

Defects and measurements accumulate without one flow for loss, priority, owner, effect, and recurrence.

FIELD GAP
OPERATIONS

Machine status is separated from response priority

Run, wait, stop, and cycle are visible, while loss criteria and the next owned task are prepared separately.

FIELD GAP
LOGISTICS

Material demand is disconnected from input confirmation

Request, priority, dispatch, arrival, and input confirmation are split across people and tools.
